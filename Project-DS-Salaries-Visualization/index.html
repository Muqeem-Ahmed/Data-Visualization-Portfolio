The Data

Dataset Overview

The Data Science Job Salaries dataset is a publicly available collection sourced from Kaggle. It covers compensation data for data science and related roles from 2020 to 2023, standardized to USD for fair global comparison.

Analysis

Key Findings

Through visualization techniques including line charts, bar charts, box plots, and choropleth maps, the following insights were uncovered from the dataset.

Average Salary by Experience Level

Senior and Executive professionals earn 2–2.5× more than Entry-level peers

🟢 Entry-Level (EN)$78,546
🔵 Mid-Level (MI)$104,526
🟠 Senior (SE)$153,051
🔴 Executive (EX)$194,931

Salary Growth Trend (2020–2023)

Mean salaries grew by +61% in just four years

📅 2020$92,303
📅 2021$94,087
📅 2022$133,339
📅 2023$149,046
🌍

US Dominates the Market

81%

Over 3,040 of 3,755 records originate from US-based companies, highlighting a strong geographic concentration in the dataset.

🏠

Remote Work Normalised

43.8%

Nearly half of all professionals in the dataset work fully remotely, up from a minority in 2020 — reflecting the post-pandemic shift.

🏢

Medium Companies Pay Most

$140K

Medium-sized companies (likely growth-stage tech firms) offer the highest median salaries, outpacing both large corporations and small startups.

👑

Top Earning Role

$375K

Data Science Tech Lead is the highest-paying role in the dataset, followed by Cloud Data Architect and Data Analytics Lead.

📈

Explosive Salary Growth

+61%

Average salaries grew from $92,303 in 2020 to $149,046 in 2023 — the steepest jump occurring between 2021 and 2022.

💼

Full-Time Dominates

99%

Full-time employment accounts for 3,718 of 3,755 records, showing that data science roles are overwhelmingly permanent positions.
